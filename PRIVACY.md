# Azki输入法隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-05

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

Azki输入法（以下简称“本应用”）包含一个 iOS / iPadOS 键盘和它的主 App。

### 概述

**本应用不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享你的任何数据。** 你输入的内容不会离开你的设备。本应用没有账号系统，没有广告，没有数据分析或遥测，也没有第三方 SDK。键盘不发起任何网络请求，所有候选词都由随 App 提供的本地词库生成。开发者无法访问你的任何数据。

### 保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据只保存在你的设备上，用于实现对应功能：

| 数据 | 用途 | 保留时间 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 设置（语言、外观、方言地区等） | 保存你的偏好 | 直到你修改或卸载 App |
| 自定义词库和快捷短语 | 提供你添加的内容 | 短语直到你删除，词库直到你恢复旧版本或卸载 App |
| 输入记忆（你选过的词、读音和选择次数） | 让常用词靠前，可以关闭 | 直到你清除或卸载 App |
| 未提交的输入草稿 | 在键盘切换时恢复输入 | 只在内存中，最长 60 秒 |
| 语音识别的文字 | 把文字从主 App 传回键盘 | 使用后删除，未使用时最长保留 60 秒 |

输入记忆只记录你实际选择的词，不记录整句内容、原始字母、光标上下文或粘贴板内容。在密码、验证码、手机号、银行卡、邮箱和网址等字段中，输入记忆不会学习。

### 按键内容

键盘会在设备上处理你的按键以生成候选词。按键内容不会被记录成日志，也不会发送到任何地方。

### 权限

- **允许完全访问：** 用于在主 App 和键盘之间共享设置、词库和短语，以及按键震动和粘贴。开启时 iOS 会显示一段系统提示，说明键盘可能传输数据，这段文字由系统统一显示；本应用的键盘不包含网络功能。不开启时，键盘仍可使用内置词库。
- **麦克风和语音识别：** 只在你使用语音输入时由主 App 申请。语音强制在设备上识别，录音和识别结果不会上传，录音也不会保存。
- **粘贴板：** 只在你主动使用粘贴功能时，通过系统粘贴控件读取。

### 删除你的数据

- 输入记忆：在主 App「输入」页点“清除本机输入记忆”。
- 快捷短语：在主 App「短语」页删除。
- 自定义词库：导入的内容可在「词库」页恢复到上一版本，全部删除需卸载 App。
- 全部数据：卸载 App 后，本应用保存的数据都会被删除。

### 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童的数据。

本政策如有变更，会更新在此页面并修改上方的更新日期。

## English

Azki Input Method ("the app") consists of an iOS / iPadOS keyboard and its main app.

### Summary

**The app does not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data.** What you type never leaves your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or telemetry, and no third-party SDKs. The keyboard makes no network requests: every suggestion comes from a dictionary bundled with the app. The developer cannot access any of your data.

### Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is used only for the feature it belongs to:

| Data | Purpose | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings (language, appearance, dialect region and so on) | Remembering your preferences | Until you change them or delete the app |
| Custom dictionary and quick phrases | Offering what you added | Phrases until you remove them; the dictionary until you restore an earlier version or delete the app |
| Input memory (words you chose, their readings and how often) | Ranking your frequent words higher; can be turned off | Until you clear it or delete the app |
| Uncommitted draft | Restoring input when the keyboard changes | In memory only, up to 60 seconds |
| Text from voice input | Passing text from the main app to the keyboard | Deleted after use, or within 60 seconds if unused |

Input memory records only the words you actually chose. It does not record whole sentences, raw letters, the text around the cursor or clipboard content. It does not learn in password, one-time code, phone number, card number, email or URL fields.

### Keystrokes

The keyboard processes your keystrokes on your device to produce suggestions. Keystrokes are not logged and are not sent anywhere.

### Permissions

- **Allow Full Access:** Used to share settings, dictionaries and phrases between the main app and the keyboard, and for key haptics and paste. When you turn it on, iOS shows a standard notice that a keyboard may transmit data. This text comes from the system; the app's keyboard contains no networking. Without it, the keyboard still works with the bundled dictionary.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition:** Requested by the main app only when you use voice input. Recognition is forced to run on your device. Audio and results are not uploaded, and audio is not saved.
- **Clipboard:** Read through the system paste control, only when you choose to paste.

### Deleting your data

- Input memory: tap "清除本机输入记忆" (Clear input memory) on the Input page of the main app.
- Quick phrases: remove them on the Phrases page of the main app.
- Custom dictionary: restore the previous version on the Dictionary page. Removing it entirely requires deleting the app.
- Everything: deleting the app removes all data it stored.

### Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with the date above updated.
